Firestick IPTV Pricing 2026: What the 3 Plan Actually Includes

By Owen Hart · August 07, 2026

Firestick IPTV pricing keeps tripping up first-time buyers — the headline number rarely matches the bill. A 1-connection plan at $23/month is a clean entry point: one screen, the full channel count, no credit card for the 7-day trial. Step up to 2 or 3 connections and the per-month figure stays flat if you pay annually; monthly billing adds a small surcharge that most reviews never mention.

If you're comparing firestick iptv pricing and plans side by side, the differences that matter are connection count, the 24-hour EPG, and whether the trial actually requires a card on file. Skip the ones that do — it's a tell. The plan that hits $23/mo is the cleanest match for a single-Firestick household testing the waters; the 5-connection tier only pays off if you actually run two TVs and a tablet at the same time.

Two things worth knowing before you commit: the 7-day refund window is real (read the fine print — it's there for a reason), and the trial does not auto-charge. Set a reminder for day 6 if you want to cancel; otherwise the first bill lands on day 8. After that, the firestick iptv subscription cost is steady at $23/mo for the basic tier, with annual saving around 20 percent.
